ExamHub Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 7, 2026
Developer: Algorise Solutions
Android package: com.algorisesolutions.examhub

ExamHub is an exam preparation app for professional, licensing, university, and self-made exams. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it is used, and the choices users have.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use ExamHub, the app may collect or store:

  • Account information such as name, email address, profile photo, user ID, and sign-in provider when you create an account or use Google sign-in.
  • Study activity such as exam attempts, answers, scores, paused sessions, history, notes, bookmarks, reports, and statistics.
  • Imported or created exam content such as text, JSON, DOC, DOCX, PDF files, custom question sets, titles, categories, language, specialty, and sharing settings.
  • Community activity such as published exams, votes, reviews, flags, usage counts, group participation, and public profile details needed to display shared content.
  • Technical information such as app version, device model, operating system, crash information, diagnostics, and basic analytics events.

How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Provide login, account, sync, backup, and multi-device access.
  • Save study progress, exam history, notes, uploaded files, reports, and custom exams.
  • Enable community features such as publishing, voting, teacher review, peer review, and group sharing.
  • Improve app quality, fix crashes, protect against abuse, and understand which features are useful.
  • Respond to user requests, reports, and support messages.

Firebase and Google Services

ExamHub may use Google Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, Analytics, and Crashlytics. These services help provide sign-in, cloud synchronization, file storage, app diagnostics, and crash reporting. Google may process data according to its own privacy and security terms.

Offline and Guest Use

Some app features may work without an account. If you use ExamHub as a guest, study activity may remain only on your device unless you later sign in and choose to synchronize supported data.

Sharing and Public Content

If you publish an exam, review content, vote, join groups, or share files through community features, selected information may become visible to other users. Do not upload files or content that you do not have the right to share.

Data Sale and Advertising

We do not sell personal information. ExamHub is intended as a paid, professional study tool and does not require third-party advertising to provide its core functionality.

Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect user information. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data Retention and Deletion

Account and study data may be retained while your account is active or as needed to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and prevent abuse. Users may request deletion of their account data and uploaded content by contacting us.

Children

ExamHub is designed for students and professionals preparing for serious exams and is not directed to children under 13. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under 13 without appropriate consent, we will take steps to delete it.

Exam Content Disclaimer

ExamHub may include official-style, user-published, peer-reviewed, teacher-reviewed, university-shared, or imported exam materials. Unless clearly stated, ExamHub is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any government ministry, testing board, university, or exam authority.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as ExamHub grows. Updates will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
