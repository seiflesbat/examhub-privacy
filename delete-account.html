Delete ExamHub Account

Effective date: May 7, 2026
Developer: Algorise Solutions
App: ExamHub

This page explains how ExamHub users can request deletion of their account and associated data.

If you use ExamHub without signing in, your study data may be stored only on your device. You can remove that local data by uninstalling the app or clearing the app storage from your Android device settings.

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Open ExamHub.
  2. Go to the Account/Profile section.
  3. Use the account deletion option if it is available in your installed app version.
  4. If the in-app option is not available, contact Algorise Solutions through https://github.com/seiflesbat.
  5. Include the email address connected to your ExamHub account and write: “Delete my ExamHub account and associated data.”

Data Deleted

When an account deletion request is completed, we delete or anonymize:

  • Account profile data such as name, email address, profile photo, and user ID where stored by ExamHub.
  • Cloud-synced study history, exam attempts, scores, paused sessions, notes, bookmarks, and statistics.
  • User-created or uploaded exam files and custom question sets stored in ExamHub cloud services.
  • Community content connected to the account where deletion is technically and legally possible.

Data That May Be Kept

Some information may be retained for a limited time when needed to:

  • Comply with legal, tax, payment, fraud-prevention, or security obligations.
  • Investigate abuse, spam, copyright violations, or harmful content reports.
  • Keep anonymized analytics or crash logs that no longer identify the user.
  • Preserve public community discussions where removing the full context would affect other users, unless removal is required by law.

Retention Period

We aim to complete verified deletion requests within 30 days. Some backup, security, or legal records may remain for up to 90 days before permanent deletion, unless a longer retention period is required by law.
